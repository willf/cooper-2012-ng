# First-line transcription review

These are candidates for checking against the 2012 Cooper Book, not confirmed
errors. Different settings may intentionally preserve different wording,
spelling, capitalization, or punctuation. No source text has been changed.

Check each item against the printed page, then record whether the transcription
is correct or needs a correction. Page suffixes `t` and `b` mean top and bottom.

## Similar openings that may be deliberate variants

weaker evidence of a transcription error.

  “While trav’ling thru this world below.” Compare with 460 and 387 only after
  the higher-priority checks; its next lines and attributed author differ.

  [558 — I’ll Praise My Master](https://willf.github.io/cooper-2012-ng/songs/558.html):**

  [547 — All Is Peace and Love](https://willf.github.io/cooper-2012-ng/songs/547.html):**
