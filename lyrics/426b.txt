Title: The Time is Rolling On
Collation: Time is Rolling On, The
Alternative Title: Shiloah
Page: 426b
Tune Composer: Waller, Thomas
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Sellers, Julia (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Hicks, Berryman T.
Lyrics Date: 1835
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

The time is swiftly rolling on
When I must faint and die,
My body to the dust return,
And there forgotten lie.

Let persecutions rage around,
And Antichrist appear,
My silent dust beneath the ground,
There’s no disturbance there.
