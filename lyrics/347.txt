Title: It Is a Wonder If I Am Saved
Alternative Title: ’Tis Wonder
Page: 347
Tune Composer: Rees, John Palmer
Tune Composition Date: 1857
Alto Composer: Floyd, Minnie (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Hollyday (Rev.)
Lyrics Date: 1929
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

O ’tis a glorious mystery,
’Tis a wonder,
That I should ever saved be!
’Tis a wonder.

Chorus:

’Twill be a wonder, wonder, a wonder,
’Twill be a wonder be if I am saved.
