Title: Messiah’s Praise
Page: 394
Tune Composer: R. F. M. Mann and James A. Spark
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Miss Dosia McCoy
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Benjamin Rhodes
Lyrics Date: 1787
Meter: Irregular

________________________________________________________________________________

My heart and voice I raise
To spread Messiah’s praise,
Let all repeat,
The universal Lord.
Praise Him, Let all praise Him,
By whose almighty word,
Creation rose in form complete
