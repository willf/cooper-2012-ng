Title: We Will Sing with Angels There
Collation: We Will Sing with Angels There
Page: 572
Tune Composer: Givens, D. J.
Tune Composition Date: 1926
Lyrics: Adkins, H. R.
Lyrics Date: 1917
Meter: Particular Meter: 7,6,8,6

________________________________________________________________________________

There is a place called heaven,
The homeland of the soul,
The angels will be singing there,
While endless ages roll.

Chorus:

We will sing with the angels there,
In that happy home so fair,
In heaven above where all is love,
We will sing with the angels there.

That home is made of jasper,
Its walks are laid with gold,
The beauties of that heav’nly place,
Have never yet been told.

(Chorus)

There we shall live forever,
In that eternal home,
We’ll meet our Savior face to face,
Around that happy throne.

(Chorus)
