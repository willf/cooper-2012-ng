Title: All Is Well
Page: 122
Tune Composer: White, Jesse T. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Pious Songs
Lyrics Date: 1836
Meter: Particular Meter: 10,6,10,6,8,8,8,6

What’s this that steals, that steals upon my frame?
Is it death? Is it death?
That soon will quench, will quench this mortal flame?
Is it death? Is it death?
If this be death I soon shall be,
From ev’ry pain and sorrow free,
I shall the King of glory see.
All is well! All is well!

Weep not, my friends, my friends, weep not for me,
All is well! All is well!
My sins forgiv’n, forgiv’n, and I am free,
All is well! All is well!
There’s not a cloud that doth arise,
To hide my Jesus from my eyes,
I soon shall mount the upper skies.
All is well! All is well!
