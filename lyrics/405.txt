Title: Children of the Heavenly King
Alternative Title: The Marcellas
Page: 405
Tune Composer: Dumas, Edmund (Eld.)
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Poyner, B. P.
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Cennick, John
Lyrics Date: 1742
Meter: 7s (7,7,7,7)

Children of the heav’nly King,
As we journey sweetly sing;
Sing your Saviour’s worthy praise,
Glorious in His works and ways.
