Title: News From the Regions of the Skies
Alternative Title: Oxford
Page: 306
Tune Composer: Juvenile Harmony
Tune Composition Date: 1825; Arranged by John Massengale, 1850
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1706
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Shepherds, rejoice! lift up your eyes,
And send your fears away;
News from the regions of the skies,
A Savior’s born today!
Jesus, the God, whom angels fear,
Comes down to dwell with you;
Today He makes his entrance here,
But not as monarchs do!
