Title: Still Better
Page: 455b
Tune Composer: Arranged by Israel Mayfield and J. L. Meggs
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Miss Minnie Floyd
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: The Sacred Harp
Lyrics Date: 1869
Meter: 8s,7s Double (8,7,8,7,8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Teach me how to do my duty
In the service of my Lord,
Then when time with me is over,
I’ll receive the great reward,
And with Jesus, Christ, my Saviour,
I shall live in endless day,
When my sorrows and my toiling
Shall be banished far away.
