Title: Vesper
Collation: Vesper
Page: 455t
Tune Composer: Letson, C. F.
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Duncan, Mary Lundie
Lyrics Date: 1839
Meter: 8s,7s (8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Savior, tender Shepherd, hear me,
Bless thy little lamb tonight;
Through the darkness be Thou near me,
Watch me ’till the morning light.

All this day Thy hand has led me,
And I thank Thee for Thy care:
Kindly Thou hast clothed me, fed me’
Listen to my ev’ning pray’r.
