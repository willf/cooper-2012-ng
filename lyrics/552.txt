Title: And Live Through Endless Day
Page: 552
Tune Composer: George L. Beck
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1707
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Should earth against my soul engage,
And fiery darts be hurled,
Then I can smile at Satan’s rage,
And face a frowning world.
So pilgrims, we are marching home,
On earth no more to stay,
Long for the welcome call to come,
And live thro’ endless day.
