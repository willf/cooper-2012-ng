Title: Eternal Spirit
Alternative Title: Uxbridge
Page: 265b
Tune Composer: Lowell Mason
Tune Composition Date: 1830
Lyrics: William Bengo Collyer
Lyrics Date: 1812
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Thanks to the hand that set us free,
Eternal Spirit, thanks to thee,
Whose pow'r, resistless, unconfined,
Subdues the passion of the mind.

Subdued by love, and taught of God,
Rejoicing in redeeming blood,
We press to find that happy shore,
Where sin and sorrow reign no more.
