Title: Marthasville
Collation: Marthasville
Page: 273
Tune Composer: Lee, David I.
Tune Composition Date: 1995
Lyrics: Lee, David I.
Lyrics Date: 1995
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Before the judgment bar of God,
I soon must trembling stand.
My worthless soul must answer all
Thy holy laws demand.
Thy justice, Lord, condemns my soul
For all eternity;
Yet I would plead a Savior’s grace
To set a sinner free.

Behold how great my need, O Lord,
And how extreme my case.
My doom pronounced would flash like fire
And drive me from Thy face.
But yet I call upon Thy Son
Who died on Calvary;
Again I plead a Savior’s grace
To set a sinner free.
