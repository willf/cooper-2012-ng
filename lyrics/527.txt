Title: Heavenly Grace
Page: 527
Tune Composer: Thomas Willard Loftin
Tune Composition Date: 1911
Lyrics: Thomas Willard Loftin
Lyrics Date: 1911
Meter: 8s,7s (8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Lord, when together here we meet,
And taste Thy heav’nly grace,
Thy smiles are so divinely sweet,
We’re loathe to leave the place.

Yet, Father, since it is Thy will,
That we must part again,
Oh let Thy gracious pardon still
With ev’ry soul remain.
