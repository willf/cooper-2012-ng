Title: Come, We That Love the Lord
Collation: Come, We That Love the Lord
Alternative Title: Webster
Page: 31b
Tune Composer: Southern Harmony
Tune Composition Date: 1835
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac
Lyrics Date: 1707
Meter: Short Meter (6,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Come, we that love the Lord,
And let our joys be known;
Join in a song with sweet accord,
And thus surround the throne.

The hill of Zion yields
A thousand sacred sweets,
Before we reach the heav’nly fields,
Or walk the golden streets.

Then let our songs abound,
And ev’ry tear be dry;
We’re marching thru Immanuel’s ground
To fairer worlds on high.
