Title: The God of Love
Collation: God of Love, The
Page: 548
Tune Composer: Beck, George L.
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Meter: Long Particular Meter (8,8,8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

To thee the gracious Lord and King,
We lift our voice aloud and sing,
Thou art the God of host above,
On earth you are the God of Love.
High was Thy throne e’er heav’n was made,
Or earth Thy humble footstool laid.
