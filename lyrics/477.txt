Title: Let the Saints Appear
Collation: Let the Saints Appear
Page: 477
Tune Composer: Thompson, William R. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1949
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac (1st Verse & Chorus)
Lyrics Date: 1719 (1st Verse & Chorus)
Meter: Common Meter Half (8.6)

________________________________________________________________________________

With rev’rence let the saints appear,
And bow before the Lord.

Chorus:

His high command with rev’rence hear,
And tremble at His Word.
And tremble at His feet because
The message we have heard.

Let angels bow before the Lord,
And praise His holy name.

(Chorus)
