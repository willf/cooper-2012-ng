Title: Praising Jesus
Page: 538
Tune Composer: S. J. Byrd
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Meter: 8s,7s Double (8,7,8,7,8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Come, dear brethren, praise your Jesus,
Praise Him, praise Him evermore;
May His great love now constrain us,
His great name for to adore.
Oh, then let us join together,
Crown of glory to obtain,
He is Christ and our salvation,
He is our exalted King.
