Title: Mount Pleasant
Alternative Title: Short Version
Page: 372
Tune Composer: Deolph
Tune Composition Date: 1793; Arranged by B. P. Poyner, 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1709
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

There is a home not made with hands,
Eternal and on high;
And here my spirit waiting stands
'Till God shall bid it fly.
