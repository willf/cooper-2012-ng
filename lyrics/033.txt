Title: Gathering Home
Page: 33
Tune Composer: Thomas Jefferson Allen
Tune Composition Date: 1902
Alto Composer: Maud Lee Allen
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1707 (1st Verse); Gurdon Robins, 1843 (2nd Verse)
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

There is a land of pure delight,
Where we shall be gathered home;
Infinite day excludes the night,
Where we shall be gathered home.

Chorus:

Where we shall be gathered home,
To meet with the Savior there,
And all the sainted, blood-washed throng,
Where we shall be gathered home.

Its skies are not like earthly skies,
Where we shall be gathered home;
It hath no need of sun to rise,
Where we shall be gathered home.

(Chorus)
