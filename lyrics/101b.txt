Title: Holy City

Page: 101b
Tune Composer: White, Benjamin Franklin
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Mead’s General Selection
Lyrics Date: 1807
Meter: 7s,6s Double (7,6,7,6,7,6,7,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

There is a holy city,
A happy world above,
Beyond the starry regions,
Built by the God of love.
An everlasting temple,
And saints array’d in white;
They serve their great Redeemer,
And dwell with Him in light.
