Title: The Cause of Christ
Page: 325
Tune Composer: Elias Lafayette King
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Miss Lessie Green
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Jared B. Waterbury
Lyrics Date: 1828
Meter: Particular Meter: 7,7,7,5,7,7,7,5

________________________________________________________________________________

Who the cause of Christ would yield?
Who would leave the battlefield?
Who would cast away his lot?
Let him basely go!
Who for Zion’s King will stand?
Who will join the faithful band?
Let him come with heart and hand,
Let him face the foe!
