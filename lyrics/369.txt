Title: Send a Blessing
Page: 369
Tune Composer: Benjamin Franklin White and Dr. Lewis L. Ledbetter
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto: added in 1902
Lyrics: John Gambold
Lyrics Date: 1748
Meter: 10s,11s (10,11,10,11)

________________________________________________________________________________

O tell me no more of this world’s vain store,
The time for such trifles with me now is o’er.
A country I’ve found, where true joys abound,
To dwell I’m determined on that happy ground.

Chorus:

Send a blessing just now.

No mortal will know what Christ doth bestow,
What life, strength, and comfort do after Him go;
Lo! onward I move to see Christ above,
None guesses how wondrous my journey will prove.

(Chorus)
