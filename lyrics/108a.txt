Title: Weeping Sinners
Page: 108t
Tune Composer: John Palmer Rees
Tune Composition Date: 1868
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Oliver Holden
Lyrics Date: 1806
Meter: 7s (7,7,7,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Weeping sinners, dry your tears,
Jesus on the throne appears;
Mercy comes with balmy wings,
Bids you His salvation sing.

Peace He brings you by His death,
Peace He speaks with ev’ry breath;
Can you slight such heav’nly charms?
Flee, O flee to Jesus’ arms.
