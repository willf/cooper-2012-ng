Title: Our King Above the Sky
Page: 570
Tune Composer: Beck, George L.
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac (1st Half)
Lyrics Date: 1707 (1st Half); 1719 (2nd Half)
Meter: Long Meter Double (8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Life is the time to serve the Lord,
The time to seek the great reward,
And while the lamp holds out to burn,
The vilest sinner may return.
The God we worship here below,
Will guide us all until we die,
He is our Saviour as we go,
Our King above the sky.
