Title: Sweet Canaan
Page: 87
Tune Composer: King, Elisha J. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Mead’s General Selection
Lyrics Date: 1807
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

O who will come and go with me?
I am bound for the land of Canaan.
I’m bound fair Canaan’s land to see,
I am bound for the land of Canaan.

Chorus:

O Canaan, sweet Canaan,
I’m bound for the land of Canaan,
Sweet Canaan, ’tis my happy home;
I am bound for the land of Canaan.

I’ll join with those who’re gone before,
I am bound for the land of Canaan.
Where sin and sorrow are no more,
I am bound for the land of Canaan.

(Chorus)
