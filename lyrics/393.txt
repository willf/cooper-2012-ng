Title: I’m Wandering To and Fro
Page: 393
Tune Composer: Arranged by Wilson Marion Cooper
Tune Composition Date: 1907
Lyrics: L. Boyden
Lyrics Date: 1860
Meter: 6s (6,6,6,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

I’m wand’ring to and fro
In this wide world of woe,
Where streams of sorrow flow,
And then I’m going home.

Chorus:

We’ll camp a while in the wilderness,
And then we’re going home.

And when my faith is tried,
In Him I will confide,
And all the storms outride,
And then I’m going home.

(Chorus)
