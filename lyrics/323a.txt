Title: Mullins
Page: 323t
Tune Composer: Eld. Edmund Dumas
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Peak's New Collection of Hymns and Spiritual Songs
Lyrics Date: 1793
Meter: 8s,7s (8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Jesus, grant us all a blessing;
Send it down, Lord, from above:
May we all go home a-praising,
And rejoicing in Thy love.

Chorus:

Farewell, breth'ren, farewell, sisters,
Till we all shall meet again.

Jesus, pardon all our folly,
Since together we have been;
Make us humble, make us holy,
Cleanse us all from ev'ry sin.

(Chorus)
