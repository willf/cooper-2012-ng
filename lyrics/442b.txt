Title: Pickard’s Hymn
Page: 442b
Tune Composer: Thomas Waller
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Miss Minnie Floyd
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: John Newton
Lyrics Date: 1779
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

In vain my fancy tries to paint
The moment after death;
The glories that surround the saints
When yielding up their breath.
One gentle sigh their fetter breaks,
We scarce can say their gone,
Before the willing spirit takes
Her mansion near the throne.
