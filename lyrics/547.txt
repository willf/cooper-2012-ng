Title: All Is Peace and Love
Page: 547
Tune Composer: Helms, W. A.
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Meter: 8,8,8,6 (8,8,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

We have our troubles here below,
And travel thro’ this world of woe,
To that bright world where angels dwell,
Where all is peace and love.

Chorus:

Where all is peace and love,
To that bright world where angels dwell,
Where all is peace and love.
Where all is peace and love,
To guide my weary feet above,
Where all is peace and love.

I feel no way like getting tired,
I’m trusting in His holy word,
To guide my weary feet above,
Where all is peace and love.

(Chorus)
