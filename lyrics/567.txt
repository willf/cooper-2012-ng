Title: Child of Thine
Collation: Child of Thine
Page: 567
Tune Composer: Faust, Bascom Franklin (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac
Lyrics Date: 1907
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Father, I wait before Thy throne,
Call me a child of Thine,
Send down the Spirit of Thy Son,
To form my heart divine.

Then shed Thy promised love abroad,
And make my comfort strong.
Then shall I say my Father God,
With an unwav’ring tongue.
