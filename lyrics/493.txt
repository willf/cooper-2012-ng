Title: Where Heavenly Joy Imparts
Collation: Where Heavenly Joy Imparts
Page: 493
Tune Composer: Givens, W. C.
Tune Composition Date: 1909
Lyrics: Medley, Samuel
Lyrics Date: 1789
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

O what amazing words of grace
Are in the gospel found;
Suited to ev’ry sinner’s case
Who knows the joyful sound.

Chorus:

Come, then, with all your wants and wounds,
Your ev’ry burden bring;
Here love, unchanging love, abounds,
A deep celestial spring.

This spring with living water flows,
And heav’nly joy imparts;
Come, thirsty souls, your wants disclose,
And drink with thankful hearts.

(Chorus)
