Jesus, my all, to heav’n is gone,
Glory hallelujah!
He whom I fix my hopes upon,
Glory hallelujah!

Chorus:

I want a seat in paradise,
Glory hallelujah!
I love that union never dies,
Glory hallelujah!

His track I see and I’ll pursue,
Glory hallelujah!
The narrow way ’till Him I view,
Glory hallelujah!
