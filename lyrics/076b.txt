Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Edward Trivett, c1772; Alt. Purify’s Selection of Hymns and Spiritual Songs

’Tis my desire with God to walk
’Till the warfare is over, hallelujah,
And with His children pray and talk
’Till the warfare is over, hallelujah,

Chorus:

Cry Amen, pray on
’Till the warfare is over, hallelujah.

’Tis my desire above the rest
’Till the warfare is over, hallelujah,
To lean my head on Jesus’ breast
’Till the warfare is over, hallelujah,
