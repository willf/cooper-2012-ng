Title: Almighty Father
Collation: Almighty Father
Page: 456
Tune Composer: White, Benjamin Franklin (arranger); Willard, Karen (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1869; 2012 (arrangement)
Lyrics: Hymns for Sunday Schools
Lyrics Date: 1842
Meter: 8s,7s Double (8,7,8,7,8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Up to Thee, Almighty Father,
Ancient of eternal days;
Throned in uncreated glory,
Hear us while our songs we raise.
Praise for Thy unceasing bounty,
Poured with an indulgent hand,
Praise for blessings still increasing,
Crowning freedom’s favored land.

While a nation’s heart is leaping,
Mighty in its gushing joy,
May the song of adoration
All its grateful pow’rs employ.
Thine, O Lord, shall be the Kingdom,
Thine the pow’r and glory be;
Thine through endless ages rolling,
Thine throughout eternity.
