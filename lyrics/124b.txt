Title: The Dying Minister
Page: 124b
Tune Composer: Eld. Edmund Dumas
Tune Composition Date: 1854
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Berryman T. Hicks
Lyrics Date: 1835
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

The time is swiftly rolling on
When I must faint and die;
My body to the dust return,
And there forgotten lie.

Let persecution rage around,
And antichrist appear;
My silent dust beneath the ground,
There's no disturbance there.
