Title: Elder
Page: 452
Tune Composer: Wynne, M. Mark
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Lyrics: American Sunday School Psalmody
Lyrics Date: 1832
Meter: 7s Double (7,7,7,7,7,7,7,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Gently glides the stream of life
Oft along the flow’ry vale,
Or impetuous down the cliff,
Rushing, roars, when storms assail.
Life’s an ever varied flood,
Always rolling toward its sea;
Slow, or quick, or mild or rude,
Tending to eternity.
