Title: Do Not I Love Thee
Collation: Do Not I Love Thee
Alternative Title: Detroit
Page: 68t
Tune Composer: Bradshaw, William (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1820
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Doddridge, Philip
Lyrics Date: 1755
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Do not I love Thee, O my Lord?
Behold my heart, and see;
And turn each worthless idol out,
That dares to rival Thee.

Do not I love Thee from my soul?
Then let me nothing love:
Dead be my heart to ev’ry joy,
Which Thou dost not approve.

Hast Thou a lamb in all Thy flock,
I would disdain to feed?
Hast Thou a foe before whose face,
I fear Thy cause to plead?

Thou know’st I love Thee, dearest Lord,
But O, I long to soar
Far from the sphere of mortal joys,
That I may love Thee more.
