Title: The Sinner Must Be Born Again
Alternative Title: Ganges
Page: 295
Tune Composer: Bridgewater Collection
Tune Composition Date: 1802
Treble Composer: Genuine Church Music
Treble Composition Date: 1832
Alto Composer: Genuine Church Music
Alto Composition Date: 1832
Lyrics: Occom, Samson (Rev.)
Lyrics Date: 1774
Meter: Common Particular Meter (8,8,6,8,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Awaked by Sinai’s awful sound,
My soul in guilt and thrall I found,
And knew not where to go;
O’erwhelmed with sin, with anguish slain,
The sinner must be born again
Or sink in endless woe.
