Title: My Last Moments
Page: 445b
Tune Composer: Rees, John Palmer
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Lyrics: Shindler, Mary Stanley Bunce Dana
Lyrics Date: 1840
Meter: Short Meter (6,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

When the last moments come,
O watch my dying face,
And catch the bright, seraphic gleam,
Which o’er each feature plays.
