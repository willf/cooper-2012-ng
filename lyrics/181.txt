Title: The Gospel Invitation
Collation: Gospel Invitation, The
Page: 181
Tune Composer: Shaw, J. M.
Tune Composition Date: 1902
Alto Composer: Allen, Thomas Jefferson
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Come, sinner, to the gospel feast,
O come without delay,
For there is room in Jesus’ breast
For all who will obey;
There’s room in God’s eternal love
To save thy precious soul,
Room in the Spirit’s grace above
To heal and make thee whole.
