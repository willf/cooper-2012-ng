Title: Liberty
Page: 137
Tune Composer: Stephen Jenks
Tune Composition Date: 1800
Alto Composer: Miss Minnie Floyd
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

No more beneath th’ oppressive hand
Of tyranny we groan,
Behold the smiling, happy land,
That freedom calls her own.
