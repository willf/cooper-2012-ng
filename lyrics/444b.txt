Title: The Teacher’s Farewell
Collation: Teacher’s Farewell, The
Page: 444b
Tune Composer: Dumas, Edmund (Eld.) (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1858
Alto Composer: McCoy, Lina (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Dumas, Edmund (Eld.)
Lyrics Date: 1858
Meter: Short Meter (6,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Our school now closes out,
And we today must part;
How sad the thought to part with you,
I hope we’ll meet again.

Chorus:

O let us meet in heav’n,
The Christian’s happy home,
The house above, where all is love,
There’ll be no parting there.

(Chorus)

You’ve been so kind to me,
How can I bear the thought
To part with you, It greives my heart,
Perhaps to meet no more.

(Chorus)

Wherever you may go,
Dear students, think of me,
O pray for me where’er you go,
That we may meet in heav’n.

(Chorus)
