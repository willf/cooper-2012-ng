Title: Palmetto
Page: 54t
Tune Composer: Dr. William Hauser
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Lyrics: James Allen
Lyrics Date: 1757; Alt. by Walter Shirley, 1774 (1st Verse); Christian Harmony, 1958 (2nd Verse)
Meter: 8s,7s Double (8,7,8,7,8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Sweet the moments, rich in blessing,
Which before the cross I spend,
Life, and health, and peace possessing,
From the sinner's dying friend.
Here I'll sit forever viewing
Mercy's streams in streams of blood;
Precious drops, my soul bedewing,
Plead and claim my peace with God.

Had I heart to hold its pleasure,
Had I tongue to tell its bliss;
Could I share with all my treasure,
O that all my friends were his.
Nothing else could quite so charm me,
Nor my ev'ry idol hold.
Satan now no more can harm me,
I've a home in realms of gold.
