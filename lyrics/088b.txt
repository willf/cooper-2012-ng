Title: Mount Zion
Collation: Mount Zion
Page: 88b
Tune Composer: Massengale, John
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Wesley, Charles
Lyrics Date: 1739
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

O for a thousand tongues to sing
My great Redeemer’s praise,
The glories of my God and King,
The triumphs of His grace.

Chorus:

O Christians, praise Him,
Methinks I hear the gospel sounding
For more volunteers.
