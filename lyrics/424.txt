Title: Sweet Union
Page: 424
Tune Composer: John P. Rees
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Mrs. Anna L. (Cooper) Blackshear
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Samuel Medley
Lyrics Date: 1782
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Awake my soul, in joyful lays,
O glory hallelujah!
And sing thy great Redeemer’s praise;
Don’t you love God, glory hallelujah!

Chorus:

There’s union in heav’n, and there’s union in my soul;
O glory hallelujah,
Sweet music in Zion’s beginning to roll,
Don’t you love God, glory hallelujah
