Title: David’s Lamentation
Page: 239
Tune Composer: Billings, William
Tune Composition Date: 1778
Alto Composer: Kentucky Harmony
Alto Composition Date: 1816
Lyrics: Holy Bible: II Samuel 18:33
Meter: Prose

________________________________________________________________________________

David, the king, was grieved and moved
He went to his chamber and wept;
And as he went, he wept and said:
“O my son,
Would to God I had died,
For thee, O Absalom, my son!”
