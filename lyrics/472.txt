Title: The Sinner’s Friend
Collation: Sinner’s Friend, The
Page: 472
Tune Composer: Rees, John Palmer
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac
Lyrics Date: 1709
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

He dies! the friend of sinners dies;
And He died on the cross for sinners;
Lo! Salem’s daughters weep around;
And He died on the cross for sinners.

Chorus:

I love my Lord, for He first loved me,
And He died on the cross for sinners.

Come, saints, and drop a tear or two,
For He died on the cross for sinners;
For him who groaned beneath your load!
And He died on the cross for sinners.

(Chorus)

He shed a thousand drops for you,
And He died on the cross for sinners;
A thousand drops of richer blood!
And He died on the cross for sinners.

(Chorus)
