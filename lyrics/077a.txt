Title: The Child of Grace
Collation: Child of Grace, The
Page: 77t
Tune Composer: King, Elisha J. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Wesley, Charles
Lyrics Date: 1759
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

How happy’s ev’ry child of grace
Who knows his sins forgiv’n!
‘This earth,’ he cries, ‘is not my place,
I seek my home in heav’n.
A country far from mortal sight,
Yet O by faith I see
The land of rest the saints’ delight,
The heav’n prepared for me.’

O, what a blessed hope is ours
While here on earth we stay.
We more than taste the heav’nly pow’rs
And antedate that day.
We feel the resurrection near,
Our life in Christ concealed,
And with His glorious presence here
Our earthen vessels filled.
