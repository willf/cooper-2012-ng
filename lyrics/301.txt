Title: God’s Promise
Page: 301
Tune Composer: George L. Beck
Tune Composition Date: 1948; Arranged by Robert Lee Vaughn and Karen Willard, 2011
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

There is a thought that comes to me,
That time cannot erase,
Reminding me of God’s own Son,
Who saves and keeps by grace,
And so I journey down Life’s Road,
Tho’ short may be the Race,
I’ll keep on working for my Lord,
Who saves and keeps by grace.
