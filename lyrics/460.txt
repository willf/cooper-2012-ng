Title: My Soul Abounds with Joy
Collation: My Soul Abounds with Joy
Page: 460
Tune Composer: Beck, George L.
Tune Composition Date: 1929
Lyrics: Beck, George L.
Lyrics Date: 1929
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

While trav’ling thro’ this world below
Where sore affliction come
My soul abounds with joy to know
That I will rest at home.
Yes, when my eyes are closed in death,
My body cease to roam,
I’ll bid farewell to all below,
And meet my friends at home.
