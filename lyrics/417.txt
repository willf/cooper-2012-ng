Title: Weeping Pilgrim
Collation: Weeping Pilgrim
Page: 417
Tune Composer: Rees, John Palmer
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Rees, John Palmer
Lyrics Date: 1859
Meter: Irregular

________________________________________________________________________________

You may tell them, father,
When you see them,
I’m a poor mourning pilgrim,
I’m bound for Canaan’s land;
You may tell them, mother,
When you see them,
I’m a poor mourning pilgrim,
I’m bound for Canaan’s land.

Chorus:

I weep, and I mourn,
And I move slowly on,
I’m a poor mourning pilgrim,
I’m bound for Canaan’s land.

You may tell them, brothers,
When you see them,
I’m a poor mourning pilgrim,
I’m bound for Canaan’s land;
You may tell them, sisters,
When you see them,
I’m a poor mourning pilgrim,
I’m bound for Canaan’s land.

(Chorus)
