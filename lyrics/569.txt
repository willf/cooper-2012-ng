Title: He Leads Me All the Way
Page: 569
Tune: Dennis Wilson Rountree
Lyrics: Dennis Wilson Rountree
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

My Savior leads me ev'ry day,
Through life's long weary road;
He guides my feet along the way,
And to the throne of grace.
My Savior leads me ev'ry day,
Thro' ev'ry trial and fear;
Is ever near, my heart to cheer,
He leads me all the way.
