Title: Can I Leave You?
Collation: Can I Leave You?
Page: 395t
Tune Composer: Rees, John Palmer (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Watson, J. W.
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Smith, Samuel Francis (D. D.)
Lyrics Date: 1831
Meter: 8s,7s (8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Yes, my native land, I love thee,
All thy scenes I love them well,
Friends, connections, happy country,
Can I bid you all farewell?

Chorus:

Can I leave you?
Far in heathen lands to dwell?

Home, thy joys are passing lovely,
Joys no stranger’s heart can tell;
Happy home indeed I love thee;
Can I, can I, say, “Farewell”?

(Chorus)
