Title: The Last Words of Copernicus
Page: 112
Tune Composer: Lancaster, Sarah (Miss)
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Floyd, Minnie (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Doddridge, Philip
Lyrics Date: 1755
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Ye golden lamps of heav’n, farewell,
With all your feeble light,
Farewell, thou ever changing moon,
Pale empress of the night.
And thou, refulgent orb of day,
In brighter flames arrayed,
My soul which springs beyond thy sphere,
No more demands thy aid.
