Title: Longing for Heaven
Collation: Longing for Heaven
Alternative Title: Panting for Heaven
Page: 384
Tune Composer: Brown, S. M.
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Floyd, Minnie (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: DeFleury, Maria
Lyrics Date: 1791
Meter: 8s Double (8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

O when will the period appear,
When I shall unite in your song?
I’m weary of lingering here,
And I to your Savior belong.

Chorus:

I’m fettered and chained up in clay,
I struggle and pant to be free,
I long to be soaring away,
My God and my Savior to see.

Ye angels who stand round the thrown,
And view my Savior’s face,
In your music make Him be known,
And tune your soft harps to His praise.

(Chorus)
