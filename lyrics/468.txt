Title: Sweet Thought
Page: 468
Tune Composer: Eld. Robert Lee Vaughn
Tune Composition Date: 1982
Lyrics: Eld. Robert Lee Vaughn
Lyrics Date: 1982
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,6,8,8,6

________________________________________________________________________________

How sweet to think He died for me
Upon the cruel tree;
It brings me pain and agony,
But, yet it brings me ecstasy.
Just think He died for me.

A wretched sinner vile am I
For whom the Lord did die.
He sent His own begotten Son,
Redemption paid for everyone.
Just think He died for you.
