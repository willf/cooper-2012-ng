Title: Hark! from the Tomb
Collation: Hark! from the Tomb
Alternative Title: Plenary
Page: 162
Tune Composer: Clark, A. C. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1839
Alto Composer: Floyd, Minnie (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac
Lyrics Date: 1707
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Hark! from the tomb a doleful sound,
Mine ears, attend the cry:
‘Ye living men, come view the ground
Where you must shortly lie.’

‘Princes, this clay must be your bed
In spite of all your pow’r;
The tall, the wise, the rev’rend head
Must lie as low as ours.’

‘Great God! is this our certain doom?
And are we now prepared?
We’re marching onward to the tomb
Where pardon is no more.’
