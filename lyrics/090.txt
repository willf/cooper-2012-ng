Title: Arise and Pray
Alternative Title: Look Out
Page: 90
Tune Composer: White, Benjamin Franklin
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Courtney’s The Christian Pocket Companion
Lyrics Date: 1805
Meter: 8s,7s Double Iambic (8,7,8,7,8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

My breth’ren all, on you I call,
Arise and look around you,
How many foes, bound to oppose,
Who’re waiting to confound you!
The gospel calls on Zion’s walls,
Shake off your sleep and slumber;
Arise and pray, we’ll win the day,
Though we are few in number.
