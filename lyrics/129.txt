Title: Heavenly Armor
Collation: Heavenly Armor
Page: 129
Tune Composer: Walker, William
Tune Composition Date: 1828
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Leland, John
Lyrics Date: 1793
Meter: 7s,6s Double (7,6,7,6,7,6,7,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

And if you meet with troubles
And trials on the way,
Then cast your cares on Jesus,
And don’t forget to pray.
Gird on the heav’nly armor
Of faith, and hope, and love,
And when the combat’s ended,
He’ll take you up above.

Through grace I am determined
To conquer, though I die;
And then away to Jesus
On wings of love I’ll fly.
Farewell to sin and sorrow,
I bid you all adieu,
Then, O my friends, prove faithful,
And on your way pursue.
