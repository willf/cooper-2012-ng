Title: Let Us Sing
Collation: Let Us Sing
Page: 461
Tune Composer: Moore, W. F. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1867
Alto Composer: Floyd, Minnie (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Moore, W. F. (1st Verse); Masters, Mary
Lyrics Date: 1867 (1st Verse); 1755
Meter: 7s Half (7,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Shall we ever meet again at the house,
Then to make the chorus ring at the house of God?

Chorus:

Let us sing, sweetly sing,
At the house then we’ll sing,
Sweetly sing at the house of God.

Be the living God our Friend at the house,
Then our bliss shall never end at the house of God.

(Chorus)
