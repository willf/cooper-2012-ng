Title: White
Page: 288
Tune Composer: Eld. Edmund Dumas
Tune Composition Date: 1856
Lyrics: Dobell’s New Selection
Lyrics Date: 1806
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Ye fleeting charms of earth, farewell,
Your springs of joy are dry;
My soul now seeks another home,
A brighter world on high.

Chorus:

I’m a long time travelling here below,
I’m a long time a-travelling away from home,
I’m a long time travelling here below
To lay this body down.

Farewell, my friends, whose tender care
Has long engaged my love;
Your fond embrace I now exchange
For better friends above.

(Chorus)
