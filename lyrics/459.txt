Title: Tolling Bell
Collation: Tolling Bell
Page: 459
Tune Composer: Mann, R. F. M.
Tune Composition Date: 1868
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Shindler, Mary Stanley Bunce Dana
Lyrics Date: 1841
Meter: Particular Meter: 10,8,10,8,10,10,10,7

________________________________________________________________________________

Shed not a tear o’er your friend’s early bier,
When I am gone, when I am gone;
Smile when the slow tolling bell you shall hear,
When I am gone, when I am gone.
Weep not for me when you stand round my grave,
Think who has died His beloved to save,
Think of the crown all the ransomed shall wear,
When I am gone, I am gone.
