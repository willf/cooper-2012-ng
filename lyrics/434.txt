Title: Thus Will I Sing
Collation: Thus Will I Sing
Alternative Title: Fillmore
Page: 434
Tune Composer: Rees, John Palmer
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Lyrics: Heginbothom, Ottiwell
Lyrics Date: 1794
Meter: Long Meter Double (8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Great God, let all my tuneful pow’rs
Awake and sing Thy mighty name;
Thy hand revolves my circling hours,
Thy hand from whence my being came.
Thus I will sing ’till nature cease,
’Till sense and language are no more,
And after death Thy boundless grace
Thro’ everlasting years adore.
