Title: A Crown of Life for Me
Page: 160
Tune Composer: McDaniel, J. H.
Tune Composition Date: 1910
Meter: Particular Meter: 9,8,9,8,9,7,8,8

________________________________________________________________________________

O the day of joy will soon appear,
When the Lord shall come for my soul,
And a crown of life He will prepare,
When the Lord shall come for my soul.
When the Lord Jesus comes for my soul,
And I view cold Jordan’s stream
I’ll bid farewell to earthly care,
When the Lord shall come for my soul.
