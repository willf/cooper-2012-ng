Title: Marching to That Home
Collation: Marching to That Home
Page: 565
Tune Composer: Phillips, J. M.
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,8,4,6,8,8,6

________________________________________________________________________________

We’re marching thru this world below,
We’re trav’ling thru this world of woe,
To that bright world
Where all is peace and love.
We’re marching thru this world below,
We’re marching to a world above,
Where all is peace and love.
