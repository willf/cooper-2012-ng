Title: We’ll Meet Beyond the Grave
Page: 344
Tune Composer: Arranged by W. C. Givens
Tune Composition Date: 1907
Lyrics: W. C. Hafley
Lyrics Date: 1886
Meter: 8s,7s Iambic (8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Beyond the golden sunset sky,
Beyond the old rolling wave,
Beyond each earthly tear and sigh,
We’ll meet just beyond the grave.

Chorus:

We’ll meet, yes, we’ll meet on that shining shore,
We’ll meet in that home of love;
We’ll meet, yes, meet to part no more,
We shall meet in heaven above.

Beyond these pangs that parting brings,
Beyond all this earthly vale,
We’ll meet where joys eternal spring,
And love there shall never fail.

(Chorus)

Our refuge is the Lord our God,
His life for us all He gave,
He gave that life that we might live,
And yet He alone can save.

(Chorus)
