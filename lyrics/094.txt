Title: My Sands of Life
Alternative Title: Mulberry Grove
Page: 94
Tune Composer: John Palmer Rees
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Miss Carmei Taylor
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: H. Wood
Lyrics Date: 1869
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,8,8,8,8

________________________________________________________________________________

My sands of life are running fast;
I can’t recall one moment past:
I’ve much to do in little space,
Oh, can I yet be saved by grace?
Oh, can I yet one effort make?
