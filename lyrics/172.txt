Title: Harmony
Page: 172
Tune Composer: Ellis, Abner
Tune Composition Date: 1805
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Ogilvie, John
Lyrics Date: 1762
Meter: Common Particular Meter (8,8,6,8,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Wake, all ye soaring throngs and sing,
Ye cheerful warblers of the spring,
Harmonious anthems raise,
To Him who shap’d your finer mold,
Who tipp’d your glitt’ring wings with gold,
And tuned your voice to praise.
