Title: Prospect
Page: 30b
Tune Composer: Graham
Tune Composition Date: 1835
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1707
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Why should we start or fear to die,
What tim'rous worms we mortals are;
Death 'tis the gate to endless joy,
But still we dread to enter there.

Jesus can make a dying bed
Feel soft as downy pillows are:
While on His breast I lean my head,
And breathe my life out sweetly there.
