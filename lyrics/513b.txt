Title: Whosoever Will
Page: 513b
Tune Composer: Allen, Thomas Jefferson; Smith, Stanley (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1907; 2006 (arrangement)
Alto Composer: Allen, Maud Lee
Alto Composition Date: 1907
Lyrics: Medley, Samuel
Lyrics Date: 1802
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

O what amazing words of grace
Are in the gospel found;
Suited to ev’ry sinner’s case,
Who knows the joyful sound.

Poor, sinful, needy, thirsty souls
Are freely welcomed here;
Salvation like a river rolls,
Abundant, free and clear.

‘Whoever will,’ O gracious word!
Shall of this stream partake;
Come thirsty souls, and bless the Lord,
And drink for Jesus’ sake.
