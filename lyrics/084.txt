Title: The Crucified Saviour
Page: 84
Tune Composer: Givens, D. J.
Tune Composition Date: 1909
Lyrics: Givens, D. J.
Lyrics Date: 1909
Meter: 7s Half (7,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

With a Savior crucified
On the cross of Calvary;
’Twas for you He bled and died
On the cross of Calvary.

Chorus:

Shout the chorus through
Christ who died for you
Sing of Christ who died for you;
On the cruel tree
Christ hath died for me
Sing that Jesus died for me.

’Twas for sins His life He gave
On the cross of Calvary;
And the world He died to save
On the cross of Calvary.

(Chorus)
