Title: Saint Peters
Page: 389t
Tune Composer: Sacred Harp, The
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Wesley, Charles
Lyrics Date: 1749
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

How shall a lost sinner in pain
Recover his forfeited peace?
When brought into bondage again,
What hope of a second release?

Will mercy itself be so kind
To spare such a rebel as me?
And O! can I possibly find
Such plenteous redemption in Thee?
