Title: Closer Walk With God
Page: 491
Tune Composer: D. J. Givens
Tune Composition Date: 1909
Alto Composer: Miss Claudia Wiggins
Alto Composition Date: 1909
Lyrics: William Cowper
Lyrics Date: 1779
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

O for a closer walk with God,
A calm and heav’nly frame;
A light to shine upon the road,
That leads me to the Lamb.

Chorus:

I want my friends to go with me
To my eternal home;
I want my friends to go with me
To the New Jerusalem.

Where is the blessedness I knew,
When first I saw the Lord?
Where is the soul’s refreshing view
Of Jesus and His word.

(Chorus)
