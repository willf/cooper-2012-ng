Title: Trusting Jesus
Collation: Trusting Jesus
Page: 573
Tune Composer: Givens, W. C. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: Stites, Edgar Page
Lyrics Date: 1876
Meter: 7s (7,7,7,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Brightly doth His spirit shine,
Into this poor heart of mine,
While He leads I cannot fall,
Trusting Jesus, that is all.

Chorus:

Trusting as the moments fly,
Trusting as the days go by,
Trusting Him whate e’re may fall,
Trusting Jesus, that is all.

Singing if my way is clear,
Praying if the path is drear,
If in danger for Him call,
Trusting Jesus, that is all.

(Chorus)

Trusing Him while life shall last,
Trusting Him till earth is past,
’Till within the jasper walls,
Trusting Jesus, that is all.

(Chorus)
