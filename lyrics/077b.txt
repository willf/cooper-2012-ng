Title: The Saviour’s Word
Collation: Saviour’s Word, The
Alternative Title: Talbotton
Page: 77b
Tune Composer: King, Elisha J.
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Cowper, William
Lyrics Date: 1768
Meter: 7s (7,7,7,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Hark! my soul, it is the Lord,
’Tis the Savior, hear His word;
Jesus speaks, He speaks to thee,
‘Say, poor sinner, lov’st thou me?’

Lord, it is my chief complaint
That my love is weak and faint;
Yet I love Thee, and adore;
O for grace to love Thee more!
