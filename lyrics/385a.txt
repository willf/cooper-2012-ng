Title: Fight On
Collation: Fight On
Page: 385t
Tune Composer: Rees, John Palmer
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: McGee, W. L.; McCoy, W. R.
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Heath, George

Fight on, my soul, ’till death
Shall bring thee to thy God,
He’ll take thee at thy parting breath
Up to His last abode.
