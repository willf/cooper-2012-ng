Tune Composer: R. F. M. Mann
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Miss Minnie Floyd
Alto Composition Date: 1902

Lyrics Date: 1779

Amazing grace, how sweet the sound,
That saved a wretch like me!
I once was lost, but now am found,
Was blind, but now I see.

Chorus:

Shout, shout for glory,
Shout, shout aloud for glory,
Brother, sister, mourner,
All shout glory hallelujah!
