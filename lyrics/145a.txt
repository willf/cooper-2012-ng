Title: Streams of Mercy
Alternative Title: Warrenton
Page: 145t
Tune Composer: William Walker and J. Williams
Tune Composition Date: 1835
Alto Composer: Mrs. Anna L. (Cooper) Blackshear
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Robert Robinson
Lyrics Date: 1758
Meter: 8s,7s (8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

Come, Thou Fount of ev'ry blessing,
Tune my heart to sing Thy grace;
Streams of mercy never ceasing,
Call for songs of loudest praise.

Chorus:

I am bound for the kingdom,
Will you go to glory with me?
Hallelujah, praise the Lord.
