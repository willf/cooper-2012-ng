Title: Farewell, Vain World
Collation: Farewell, Vain World
Page: 95t
Tune Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.) (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Baltimore Collection (1st & 2nd Verse); Mead’s General Selection (3rd Verse); Hunter, William (4th Verse)
Lyrics Date: 1801 (1st & 2nd Verse); 1807 (3rd Verse); 1838 (4th Verse)
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Farewell, vain world, I’m going home,
I can’t stay away;
My Savior smiles and bids me come,
I can’t stay away.

Chorus:

I cannot stay much longer here,
I can’t stay away,
For the gospel ship is passing by,
I can’t stay away.

Sweet angels beckon me away,
I can’t stay away;
To sing God’s praise in endless day,
I can’t stay away.

(Chorus)

Oh who will come and go with me,
I can’t stay away;
I’m bound fair Canaan’s land to see,
I can’t stay away.

(Chorus)

My heav’nly home is bright and fair,
I can’t stay away.
No pain nor death can enter there,
I can’t stay away.

(Chorus)
