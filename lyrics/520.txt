Title: Rest for the Soul
Page: 520
Tune Composer: Bill Hogan
Tune Composition Date: 2009
Lyrics: Bill Hogan
Lyrics Date: 2009
Meter: Particular Meter: 9,9,9

Stand ye in the way, and see, and ask
For the old paths where is the good way,
Therein shall ye find rest for your soul.

Chorus:

Come unto me all ye that labor,
Come unto me ye heavy laden,
And I will give you rest.
Take up my yoke and learn of me
For I am meek and lowly in heart,
And find rest unto your soul.

Love the Lord, your God, with all your heart
And your soul, and your mind, and your strength,
And your neighbor love, love as yourself.
