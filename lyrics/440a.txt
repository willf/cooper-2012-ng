Title: Shady Grove

Tune Composer: J. D. Arnold
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Mrs. Anna L. (Cooper) Blackshear
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

O how stupendous was the pow’r
That raised me with a word;
And ev’ry day and ev’ry hour
I lean up on the Lord.
