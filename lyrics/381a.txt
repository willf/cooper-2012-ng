Title: Never Turn Back
Page: 381t
Tune: Arranged by John Palmer Rees and Miss F. E. Parkerson
Alto Composer: W. R. McCoy
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Baltimore Collection
Lyrics Date: 1801
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

When to that blessed world I rise,
I'll never turn back any more,
And join the anthems in the skies,
I'll never turn back any more.

Chorus:

Any more, any more, any more, my Lord,
I'll never turn back any more.
