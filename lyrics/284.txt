Title: The Christian’s Rest
Page: 284
Tune Composer: Henry Goodwin Mann
Tune Composition Date: 1869; Arranged by Rev. W. A. Cumbie, 1907
Lyrics: Elizabeth King Mills
Lyrics Date: 1837
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

O land of rest, for thee I sigh,
When will the moment come
When I shall lay my armor by,
And dwell in peace at home?

Chorus:

Carry me home, Carry me home;
Carry me to my long sought home,
Where Sabbath hath no end.

No tranquil joys on earth I know,
No peaceful, shelt’ring dome;
This world’s a wilderness of woe,
This world is not my home.

(Chorus)

To Jesus Christ I sought for rest,
He bid me cease to roam,
And fly for refuge to His breast,
And He’d conduct me home.

(Chorus)
