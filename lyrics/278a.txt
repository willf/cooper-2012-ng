Title: Sweet Heaven
Collation: Sweet Heaven
Page: 278t
Tune Composer: King, Elias Lafayette
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Ecking, Samuel
Lyrics Date: 1778
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

The Lord, who built the earth and sky,
In mercy stoops to hear thy cry;
His promise all may freely claim,
Ask, and receive in Jesus’ name.

Chorus:

O heav’n, sweet heav’n, when shall I see?
O when shall I get there?

Without reserve give Christ your heart;
Let Him His righteousness impart;
Then all things else He’ll freely give;
With Him you all things shall receive.

(Chorus)
