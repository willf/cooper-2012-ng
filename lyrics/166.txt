Title: Joyful
Page: 166
Tune Composer: Arranged by Benjamin Franklin White
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Treble Composer: Elisha J. King
Treble Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: B. P. Poyner
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1724
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Am I a soldier of the cross,
A foll'wer of the Lamb?
And shall I fear to own His cause,
Or blush to speak His name?
Must I be carried to the skies
On flow'ry beds of ease?
While others fought to win the prize,
And sailed thru bloody seas?

Chorus:

O, that will be joyful, joyful, joyful!
O, that will be joyful
To meet to part no more.
To meet to part no more,
On Canaan's happy shore;
We all shall meet at Jesus' feet
With those who've gone before.
