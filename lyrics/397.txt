Title: We’ll Soon Be There
Page: 397
Tune Composer: Bradfield, Oliver (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Rees, John Palmer
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Mead’s General Selection
Lyrics Date: 1807
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

O who will come and go with me,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna,
I’m bound fair Canaan’s land to see,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna.

Chorus:

Go on, go on, we’ll soon be there,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna,
Come on, come on, we’ll soon be there,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna.

O may my soul march boldly on,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna,
And never end the blessing song,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna.

(Chorus)

O may I always persevere,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna,
And never stop ’till I get there,
We’ll shout and sing hosanna.

(Chorus)
