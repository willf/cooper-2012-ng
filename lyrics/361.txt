Title: Loving Jesus
Page: 361
Tune Composer: Giardini, Felice; White, Benjamin Franklin (arranger); Searcy, John Smith (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1722; 1850 (arrangement)
Alto Composer: Green, Lessie (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,8,7,10,8,8,7

________________________________________________________________________________

Here’s my heart, my loving Jesus,
Thou who didst from sin relieve us,
Take the purchase of Thy blood,
Loving Jesus, Thou hast bo’t a ransom!
Glory, honor, praise and power
Be unto the Lamb forever!
Glory, glory to the Lord!
