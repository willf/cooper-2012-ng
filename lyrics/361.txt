Title: Loving Jesus
Page: 361
Tune Composer: Felice Giardini
Tune Composition Date: 1722; Arranged by Benjamin Franklin White and John Smith Searcy, 1850
Alto Composer: Miss Lessie Green
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,8,7,10,8,8,7

________________________________________________________________________________

Here’s my heart, my loving Jesus,
Thou who didst from sin relieve us,
Take the purchase of Thy blood,
Loving Jesus, Thou hast bo’t a ransom!
Glory, honor, praise and power
Be unto the Lamb forever!
Glory, glory to the Lord!
