Title: Hosanna
Page: 178
Tune Composer: The American Church Harp
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: John King
Lyrics Date: 1830
Meter: 7s,6s Double (7,6,7,6,7,6,7,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

When His salvation bringing,
To Zion Jesus came,
The children all stood singing
Hosanna to His Name;
Nor did their zeal offend Him,
But as He rode along,
He let them still attend Him,
And smiled to hear their song.

And since the Lord retaineth
His love for children still,
Tho’ now as King He reigneth
On Zion’s heav’nly hill,
We’ll flock around His banner,
Who sits upon the throne,
And cry aloud ‘Hosanna
To David’s royal Son.’
