Title: Come Holy Spirit
Alternative Title: Abbeville
Page: 68b
Tune Composer: King, Elisha J. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Beddome, Benjamin
Lyrics Date: 1800
Meter: Short Meter (6,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Come, Holy Spirit, come,
With energy divine,
And on this poor benighted soul
With beams of mercy shine.

From the celestial hills
Life, light, and joy, dispense!
And may I daily, hourly feel
Thy quick’ning influence.
