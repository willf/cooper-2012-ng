Title: Soon We Shall Land on Canaan's Shore
Alternative Title: Autauga
Page: 322
Tune Composer: The Sacred Harp
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Mrs. Bamma Quick
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: John Leland
Lyrics Date: 1793
Meter: 7s,6s (7,6,7,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Oh, when shall I see Jesus,
And reign with Him above,
And from the flowing fountain
Drink everlasting love?

Chorus:

Soon we shall land on Canaan's shore,
To live for evermore.
