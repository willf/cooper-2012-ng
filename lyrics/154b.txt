Title: Eden
Page: 154b
Tune Composer: Arranged by Benjamin Franklin White
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Mrs. Anna L. (Cooper) Blackshear
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Elizabeth King Mills
Lyrics Date: 1837
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

O land of rest! for thee I sigh;
When will the moments come,
When I shall lay my armor by,
And dwell in peace at home?

Chorus:

O Eden is a land of rest,
O Eden is my home;
I’ll launch my bark on Eden’s shore,
For Eden is my home.
