Title: Eden
Page: 154b
Tune Composer: White, Benjamin Franklin (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Mills, Elizabeth King
Lyrics Date: 1837
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

O land of rest! for thee I sigh;
When will the moments come,
When I shall lay my armor by,
And dwell in peace at home?

Chorus:

O Eden is a land of rest,
O Eden is my home;
I’ll launch my bark on Eden’s shore,
For Eden is my home.
