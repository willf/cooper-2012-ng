Title: Not Made With Hands
Page: 571
Tune Composer: Arranged by Miss Avanelle Dyer
Tune Composition Date: 1904
Lyrics: John S. Brown
Lyrics Date: 1900
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Christ went a building to prepare,
Not made with hands,
And ’twill be decked with jewels rare,
Not made with hands.

Chorus:

I know, I know
I have another building;
I know, I know
Not made with hands.

Put on the armour of our God,
Not made with hands,
And take the path our captain trod,
Not made with hands.

(Chorus)

With shield of faith defy the foe,
Not made with hands,
Until you hear the trumpet blow,
Not made with hands.

(Chorus)

That city’s built with precious stone,
Not made with hands,
Within we’ll gather round the throne,
Not made with hands.

(Chorus)
