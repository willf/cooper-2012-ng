Title: God’s Eternal
Alternative Title: Complaint
Page: 357
Tune Composer: Parmenter, Ezra; Allen, Thomas Jefferson (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1791; 1893 (arrangement)
Alto Composer: Deal, W. J.
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac
Lyrics Date: 1719
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Spare us, O Lord, aloud we cry,
Nor let our sun go down so soon;
Thy years are one eternal day,
And must Thy children die so soon?
