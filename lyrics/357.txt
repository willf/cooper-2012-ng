Title: God’s Eternal
Alternative Title: Complaint
Page: 357
Tune Composer: Ezra Parmenter
Tune Composition Date: 1791; Arranged by Thomas Jefferson Allen, 1893
Alto Composer: W. J. Deal
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1719
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Spare us, O Lord, aloud we cry,
Nor let our sun go down so soon;
Thy years are one eternal day,
And must Thy children die so soon?
