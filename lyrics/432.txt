Title: The Heavenly Mansion
Collation: Heavenly Mansion, The
Page: 432
Tune Composer: Miller, William (Dr.)
Tune Composition Date: 1854
Lyrics: Hunter, William
Lyrics Date: 1838
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

My heav’nly home is bright and fair,
We’ll be gathered home;
Nor death nor sighing visit there,
We’ll be gathered home.

Chorus:

We’ll wait ’till Jesus comes,
And we’ll be gathered home.

Its glitt’ring tow’rs the sun outshines,
We’ll be gathered home;
That heav’nly mansion shall be mine,
We’ll be gathered home.

(Chorus)

My Father’s home is built on high,
We’ll be gathered home;
Above the arched and starry sky
We’ll be gathered home.

(Chorus)
