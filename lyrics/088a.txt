Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion

Jesus, my all, to heav’n is gone,
And I don’t expect to stay much longer here.
He whom I fix my hopes upon,
And I don’t expect to stay much longer here.

Chorus:

I am done with the world,
And I want to serve my Lord,
And I don’t expect to stay much longer here.
