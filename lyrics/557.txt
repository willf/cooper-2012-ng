Title: Jordan’s Banks
Collation: Jordan’s Banks
Page: 557
Tune Composer: Loftin, Thomas Willard
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: Stennett, Samuel
Lyrics Date: 1787
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

On Jordan’s stormy banks I stand,
An cast a wishful eye,
To Canaan’s fair and happy land,
Where my possessions lie.

Chorus:

I am bound, yes I’m bound,
I am bound for the promised land;
Won’t you come and go with me?
I am bound for the promised land.
