Title: Will God Forever Cast Us Off?
Alternative Title: Mear
Page: 49b
Tune Composer: A Sett of Tunes
Tune Composition Date: 1720
Alto Composer: B. P. Poyner
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1719
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Will God forever cast us off?
His wrath forever smoke
Against the people of His love,
His little chosen flock?

Where once Thy churches prayed and sang
Thy foes profanely rage;
Amid Thy gates their ensigns hang
And there their host engage.

No prophet speaks to calm our grief,
But all in silence mourn;
Nor know the time of our relief,
The hour of Thy return.

Our foes would triumph in our blood,
And make our hope their jest;
Plead Thy own cause, Almighty God,
And give Thy children rest.
