Title: The Blessed Lamb
Page: 454
Tune Composer: Sacred Harp, The; Pope, Mattie (Miss) (arranger); Willard, Karen (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1869; 1907 (arrangement); 2011 (arrangement)
Alto Composer: Floyd, Minnie (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Choice Selection of Hymns and Spiritual Songs
Lyrics Date: 1827
Meter: 8s,7s Double (8,7,8,7,8,7,8,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

See the happy spirits waiting
On the banks beyond the stream;
Sweet responses, still repeating,
Jesus, Jesus is their theme.
See! They whisper; Hark! They call me,
Sister spirit, come away;
Lo! I come, earth can’t contain me;
Hail, ye realms of endless day!

Chorus:

Hail, hail, hail, hail, hail, the blessed Lamb,
Glory, glory, glory to His name.
