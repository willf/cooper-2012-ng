Title: The Surrender
Collation: Surrender, The
Page: 381b
Tune Composer: Penick, Spencer Reed
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Mason, William
Lyrics Date: 1826
Meter: 8s,7s (8,7,8,7)

Welcome, welcome, dear Redeemer,
Welcome to this heart of mine;
Lord, I make a full surrender,
Ev’ry pow’r and thought be Thine.

Chorus:

Thine entirely, Thine entirely,
Thru eternal ages Thine,
Thine entirely, Thine entirely,
Thru eternal ages Thine.
