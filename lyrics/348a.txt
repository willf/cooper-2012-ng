Title: Happy Land
Page: 348t
Tune Composer: Arranged by Leonard P. Breedlove
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Miss Henrietta Spivey
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Andrew Young
Lyrics Date: 1838
Meter: Particular Meter: 6,4,6,4,6,7,6,4

________________________________________________________________________________

There is a happy land,
Far, far away.
Where saints in glory stand,
Bright, bright as day.
O how they sweetly sing,
Worthy is our Saviour King,
Loud let His praises ring,
Praise, praise for aye.

Come to the happy land,
Come, come away.
Why will you doubting stand?
Why yet delay?
O we shall happy be,
When from sin and sorrow free,
Lord we shall live with thee,
Blest, blest for aye.
