Title: The Christian’s Flight
Page: 466
Tune Composer: Moore, W. F.
Tune Composition Date: 1866
Lyrics: Doddridge, Philip
Lyrics Date: 1755
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,6,5,6,8,6,8,6

________________________________________________________________________________

Not many years their rounds shall roll;
Each moment brings it nigh,
When the Christian’s soul
To heav’n above shall fly.
Ye wheels of nature speed your course,
Ye mortal powers decay,
Fast as ye bring the night of death
Ye bring eternal day.
