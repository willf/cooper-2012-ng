Title: My Native Land
Collation: My Native Land
Page: 416t
Tune Composer: Helms, R. B.
Tune Composition Date: 1902
Alto Composer: Smith, Stanley
Alto Composition Date: 1992
Lyrics: Smith, Samuel Francis (D. D.) (1st Verse); Hart, Joseph (2nd Verse)
Lyrics Date: 1832 (1st Verse); 1759 (2nd Verse)
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,7,8,7,8,7

________________________________________________________________________________

Yes, my native land, I love thee,
All thy scenes I love them well;
Friends, connections, happy country,
Can I bid you all farewell?
Can I leave you, can I leave you,
Far in heathen lands to dwell?

Come, ye weary, heavy laden,
Lost and ruined by the fall,
If you tarry ’till you’re better,
You will never come at all.
Not the righteous, not the righteous,
Sinners Jesus came to save.
