Title: On To Calvary
Collation: On To Calvary
Page: 562
Tune Composer: Beck, George L.
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Treble Composer: Beck, G. L. (Mrs.)
Treble Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: Beck, George L. (1st Verse); Medley, Samuel (2nd Verse)
Lyrics Date: 1927 (1st Verse); 1776 (2nd Verse)
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

I love to think of my dear Lord,
For He has done so much for me,
He’s shown me to the narrow road,
That leads me on to Calvary.

Now, in a song of grateful praise,
To my dear Lord, my voice I’ll raise;
With all His saints, I’ll join to tell,
That Jesus hath done all things well.
