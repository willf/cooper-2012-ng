Title: Come On, My Fellow Pilgrims
Page: 471
Tune Composer: P. T. Medley
Tune Composition Date: 1907
Alto Composer: Miss Minnie Lee Scarbrough
Alto Composition Date: 1907
Lyrics: Charles' Hymns and Spiritual Songs
Lyrics Date: 1803
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,8,8,8,8,4

________________________________________________________________________________

Come on, my fellow pilgrims, come,
And let us all be hast'ning home.
We soon shall land on yon blest shore
Where pains and sorrows are no more,
There we our Jesus shall adore,
Forever blest.
