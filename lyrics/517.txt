Title: We'll Wear a Crown
Page: 517
Tune Composer: C. M. Senn
Tune Composition Date: 1909
Treble Composer: Thomas Jefferson Allen
Treble Composition Date: 1909
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

The Savior stands and pleads for thee,
Behold the dying Son,
When Jesus comes a crown we'll wear,
And we'll be gathered home.
