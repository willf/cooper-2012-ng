Title: The Glorious Light of Zion
Alternative Title: Burk
Page: 92
Tune Composer: Benjamin Franklin White
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Battle’s Collection of Hymns
Lyrics Date: 1814
Meter: 7s,6s (7,6,7,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

The glorious light of Zion
Is spreading far and wide,
And sinners now are coming
Unto the gospel tide.

Chorus:

To see the saints in glory,
And the angels stand inviting,
To welcome sinners home.

The glory of King Jesus
Triumphant doth arise,
And sinners crowd around it
With bitter groans and cries.

(Chorus)
