Title: I’m On My Journey Home
Collation: I’m On My Journey Home
Page: 207t
Tune Composer: Lancaster, Sarah (Miss)
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Spivey, Belle (Miss)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Mead’s General Selection (1st Verse); Baltimore Collection (2nd & 3rd Verse)
Lyrics Date: 1807 (1st Verse); 1801 (2nd & 3rd Verse)
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

O who will come and go with me,
I am on my journey home;
I’m bound fair Canaan’s land to see,
I am on my journey home.

Chorus:

O come and go with me,
For I’m on my journey home.

Farewell, vain world, I’m going home,
I am on my journey home;
My Savior smiles and bids me come,
I am on my journey home.

(Chorus)

Sweet angels beckon me away,
I am on my journey home;
To sing God’s praise in endless day,
I am on my journey home.

(Chorus)
