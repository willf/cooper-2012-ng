Title: Sweet Home
Page: 564
Tune Composer: George L. Beck
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: George L. Beck
Lyrics Date: 1927
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

There is a home, a peaceful home,
A home of joy and love,
And them that bear the cross below,
Shall wear a crown above.

Chorus:

My home, sweet home,
My beautiful home above,
Sweet home of love,
My beautiful home above.
