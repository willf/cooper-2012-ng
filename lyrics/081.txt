Title: Jesus Is Willing
Alternative Title: Beach Spring
Page: 81
Tune Composer: White, Benjamin Franklin
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Hart, Joseph
Lyrics Date: 1759
Meter: Particular Meter: 8,7,8,7,8,7

________________________________________________________________________________

Come, ye sinners, poor and needy,
Weak and wounded, sick and sore;
Jesus ready stands to save you,
Full of pity, love and power.
He is able, He is able,
He is willing, doubt no more.

Now, ye needy, come and welcome;
God’s free bounty glorify;
True belief and true repentance,
Ev’ry grace that brings you nigh;
Without money, without money,
Come to Jesus Christ and buy

Let not conscience make you linger,
Nor of fitness fondly dream;
All the fitness He requireth
Is to feel our need of Him.
This He gives you, this He gives you,
’Tis the Spirit’s glimmering beam.
