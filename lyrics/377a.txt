O yes, my Savior I will trust,
And tho’ my body turns to dust,

Chorus:

O what a happy time when the Christians all get home,
And we’ll shout and praise the Lamb in glory.

My spirit shall fly out and sing
Eternal praises to my King.

(Chorus)
