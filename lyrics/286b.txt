Title: Melody
Page: 286b
Tune Composer: Wyeth's Repository of Sacred Music, Part Second
Tune Composition Date: 1813; Arranged by Benjamin Franklin White, 1850
Alto Composer: Mrs. Anna L. (Cooper) Blackshear
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Charles Wesley
Lyrics Date: 1763
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

And must I be to judgment bro't,
And answer in that day,
For ev'ry vain and idle thought,
And ev'ry word I say?

Thou awful Judge of quick and dead,
The watchful pow'r bestow;
So shall I to my ways take heed,
To all I speak or do.

If now Thou standest at the door,
O let me feel Thee near!
And make my peace with God, before
I at Thy bar appear.
