Title: Solid Comfort
Collation: Solid Comfort
Alternative Title: King of Peace
Page: 74b
Tune Composer: Price, F. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1835
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Masters, Mary; Rippon’s Selection (alteration)
Lyrics Date: 1755; 1804 (alteration)
Meter: 7s (7,7,7,7)

________________________________________________________________________________

’Tis religion that can give
Sweetest pleasures while we live,
’Tis religion must supply
Solid comforts when we die.

After death, its joys will be
Lasting as eternity;
Be the living God my friend
Then my bliss shall never end.
