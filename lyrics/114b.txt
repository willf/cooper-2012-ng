Title: Fallen by the Way
Collation: Fallen by the Way
Page: 114b
Tune Composer: McCoy, W. R.
Tune Composition Date: 1902
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

I feel I’ve fallen by the way,
I feel I’m all undone;
My way gets shorter ev’ry day
To my eternal home.

Chorus:

Jesus, blessed Savior,
Hear my fainting cry;
O Jesus, be thou nearer
When I am called to die.
