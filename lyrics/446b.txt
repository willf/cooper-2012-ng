Title: Charming Sound
Collation: Charming Sound
Page: 446b
Tune Composer: Mann, R. F. M.
Tune Composition Date: 1858
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Doddridge, Philip
Lyrics Date: 1755
Meter: Short Meter (6,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Grace! ’tis a charming sound,
Harmonious to the ear;
Heav’n with the echo shall resound,
And all the earth shall hear,
Glory hallelujah!
