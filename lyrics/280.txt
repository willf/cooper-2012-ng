Title: Vain World, Be Gone
Alternative Title: Westford
Page: 280
Tune Composer: Read, Daniel
Tune Composition Date: 1785
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac
Lyrics Date: 1707
Meter: Long Meter Triple (8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Far from my thoughts, vain world, be gone,
Let my religious hours alone.
Fain would my eyes my Savior see,
I wait a visit, Lord, from Thee.
My heart grows warm with holy fire,
And kindles with a pure desire.
Come, my dear Jesus, from above,
And feed my soul with heav’nly love;
Blest Jesus, what delicious fare!
How sweet Thine entertainments are!
Never did angels taste above
Redeeming grace and dying love.
