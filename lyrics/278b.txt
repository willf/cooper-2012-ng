Title: Traveling Pilgrim
Page: 278b
Tune Composer: Rees, Henry Smith (Rev.)
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Baltimore Collection
Lyrics Date: 1801
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Farewell! vain world, I’m going home,
Where there’s no more stormy clouds to rise.
My Savior smiles, and bids me come,
Where there’s no more stormy clouds to rise.

Chorus:

To the land, To the land I am bound,
Where there’s no more stormy clouds to rise.

Sweet angels becon me away,
Where there’s no more stormy clouds to rise.
To sing God’s praise in endless day,
Where there’s no more stormy clouds to rise.

(Chorus)
