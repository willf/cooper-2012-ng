Title: Turn, Sinner, Turn
Collation: Turn, Sinner, Turn
Page: 105b
Tune Composer: King, Elisha J. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Miller, William
Lyrics Date: 1809
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Today, if you will hear His voice,
Now is the time to make your choice;
Say, will you to Mount Zion go?
Say, will you have this Christ or no?

Chorus:

O turn, sinner, turn,
May the Lord help you turn!
O turn, sinner, turn,
Why will you die?

Say, will you be forever blest,
And with this glorious Jesus rest?
Will you be saved from guilt and pain?
Will you with Christ forever reign?

(Chorus)
