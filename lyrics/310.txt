Title: The Weeping Saviour
Collation: Weeping Saviour, The
Page: 310
Tune Composer: Dumas, Edmund (Eld.) (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Watts, Isaac
Lyrics Date: 1707
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Alas! and did my Savior bleed,
And did my Sov’reign die?
Would He devote that sacred head
For such a worm as I?

Chorus:

O come, sinner, you will hear
The Savior say, ‘Weep not for me.’
See the Savior on the cross!
O sinner, hear Him cry,
‘Eloi, Eloi, lama sabacthani.’

Was it for crimes that I had done,
He groaned upon the tree?
Amazing pity, grace unknown,
And love beyond degree!

(Chorus)
