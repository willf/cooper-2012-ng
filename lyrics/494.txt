Title: If Christ Be in My Arms
Alternative Title: Grafton
Page: 494
Tune Composer: Joseph Stone
Tune Composition Date: 1793; Arranged by J. W. Livings, 1909
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1709
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Jesus the vision of Thy face,
Hath overpow'ring charms;
Scarce shall I feel death's cold embrace
If Christ be in my arms.
Then while you hear my heartstrings break,
How sweet the minutes roll,
A mortal paleness on my cheeks
And glory in my soul.
