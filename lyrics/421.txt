Title: Sweet Morning
Collation: Sweet Morning
Page: 421
Tune Composer: Rees, Henry Smith (Rev.) (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Blackshear, Anna L. (Cooper) (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Broaddus and Broaddus’ Collection of Sacred Ballads
Lyrics Date: 1790
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

The happy day shall soon appear,
And we’ll all shout together in that morning,
When Gabriel’s trumpet you shall hear,
And we’ll all shout together in that morning.

Chorus:

Sweet Morning, sweet morning,
And we’ll all shout together in that morning.

Behold the righteous marching home,
And we’ll all shout together in that morning,
And all the angels bid them come,
And we’ll all shout together in that morning.

(Chorus)
