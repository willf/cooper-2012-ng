Title: He Died on the Cross
Page: 404
Tune Composer: Stevens, D. F.
Tune Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Stevens, D. F.
Lyrics Date: 1902
Meter: Irregular

________________________________________________________________________________

Jesus came to save poor sinners,
Jesus came, Jesus came;
Jesus died to save poor sinners,
Jesus died on the cross;
He rose from the grave,
And ascended in a cloud;
He will come the second time
Without sin unto salvation,
He will come the second time.
