Title: Shepherds, Rejoice
Page: 291
Tune Composer: Leonard P. Breedlove
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Wilson Marion Cooper
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1706 (verse 1 & chorus), 1707 (verses 2 & 3)
Meter: Common Meter (8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

Shepherds, rejoice, lift up your eyes,
And send your fears away;
News from the regions of the skies,
A Saviour’s born today.

Chorus:

Jesus, the God, whom angels fear,
Come down to dwell with you,
Today He makes His entrance here,
But not as monarchs do.

He took our mortal flesh to show
The wonders of His love;
For us He paid His life below
And prays for us above.

(Chorus)

‘Forgive,’ He cries, ‘forgive their sins,
For I myself have died;’
And then He shows His open veins,
And pleads His wounded side.

(Chorus)
