Title: Roll On
Page: 275b
Tune Composer: Miss Cynthia Bass
Tune Composition Date: 1850
Alto Composer: Mrs. Anna L. (Cooper) Blackshear
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Isaac Watts
Lyrics Date: 1707
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Why should we start and fear to die?
What tim'rous worms we mortals are!
Death is the gate of endless joy,
And yet we dread to enter there.

Chorus:

Roll on, Roll on, sweet moments, roll on,
And let the poor pilgrim go home, go home.

Jesus can make a dying bed,
Feel soft as downy pillows are;
While on His breast I lean my head,
And breath my life out sweetly there.

(Chorus)
