Title: Reverential Anthem
Collation: Reverential Anthem
Page: 230
Tune Composer: King, Elisha J.
Tune Composition Date: 1844
Alto Composer: Atkinson, Clara (Mrs.)
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Holy Bible: Psalms 96:8-11, 13
Meter: Prose

________________________________________________________________________________

Give unto the Lord
The glory due unto His name;
Come into His courts;
Worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness.
Fear before Him all the earth,
He shall judge the people righteously.
Let the heav’ns rejoice,
And the earth be glad before the Lord.
For He cometh,
To judge the world with righteousness
And the people with His truth.
