Title: Christian’s Delight
Page: 429
Tune Composer: Arranged by William L. Williams
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Mrs. Anna L. (Cooper) Blackshear
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Samuel Ecking
Lyrics Date: 1778
Meter: Long Meter Half (8,8)

Peace, troubled soul, thou need not fear,
Jesus says He will be with us to the end;
Thy great Provider still is near,
Jesus says He will be with us to the end,

Chorus:

And He has been with us,
And He yet is with us,
And He’s promised to be with us to the end.

The Lord, who built the earth and sky,
Jesus says He will be with us to the end;
In mercy stoops to hear thy cry,
Jesus says He will be with us to the end,

(Chorus)

His promise all may freely claim,
Jesus says He will be with us to the end;
Ask, and receive in Jesus’ name,
Jesus says He will be with us to the end,

(Chorus)
