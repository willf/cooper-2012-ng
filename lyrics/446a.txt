Title: Sharon’s Lovely Rose
Collation: Sharon’s Lovely Rose
Page: 446t
Tune Composer: Mann, Henry Goodwin
Tune Composition Date: 1869
Lyrics: Dobell, John
Lyrics Date: 1810
Meter: Short Meter (6,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

In Sharon’s lovely rose
Immortal beauties shine;
Its sweet, refreshing fragrance shows
Its origin divine.

How blooming and how fair!
O may my happy breast
This lovely rose forever wear,
And be supremely blest.
