Title: New Hosanna
Collation: New Hosanna
Page: 412
Tune Composer: Rees, Henry Smith (Rev.) (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1859
Alto Composer: Cooper, Wilson Marion
Alto Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: Dobell’s New Selection
Lyrics Date: 1806
Meter: Long Meter (8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Wake, O my soul, and hail the morn,
For unto us a Savior’s born;
See how the angels wing there way
To usher in the glorious day.

Chorus:

Glory, glory, let us sing,
While heaven and earth His praises ring,
Hosanna to the Lamb of God.

Hark! what sweet music’what a song’
Sounds from the bright celestial throng;
Sweet song, whose melting sounds impart
Joy to each raptured list’ning heart.

(Chorus)
