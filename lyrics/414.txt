Title: Morning Sun
Page: 414
Tune Composer: Denson, Seaborn McDaniel
Tune Composition Date: 1911
Lyrics: Wolcott’s Selection of Hymns and Spiritual Songs
Lyrics Date: 1817
Meter: Long Meter Double (8,8,8,8,8,8,8,8)

________________________________________________________________________________

Youth, like the spring will soon be gone,
By fleeting time or conquering death;
Your morning sun my set at noon,
And leave you ever in the dark.
Your sparling eyes and blooming checks
Must wither like the blasted rose;
The coffin, earth, and winding sheet
Will soon your active limbs enclose.
