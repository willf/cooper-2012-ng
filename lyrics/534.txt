Title: The Saints Will Be Gathered There
Page: 534
Tune Composer: Senn, C. M.
Tune Composition Date: 1927
Lyrics: Senn, C. M.
Lyrics Date: 1927
Meter: Common Meter Double (8,6,8,6,8,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

I want my friends to go with me
To that eternal home,
Where we shall be forever there,
And sing around the throne.
The saints will all be gathered there,
For evermore to stay,
We’ll talk and sing about His love,
On that great judgment day.
