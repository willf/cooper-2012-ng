Title: Pray, Brethren, Pray
Page: 467
Tune Composer: The Minstrel of Zion
Tune Composition Date: 1845
Treble Composer: U. G. Wood Alto: Wilson Marion Cooper
Treble Composition Date: 1902
Lyrics: William Hunter
Lyrics Date: 1855
Meter: Particular Meter: 4,7,4,6

________________________________________________________________________________

Pray, brethren, pray,
The pray’r of faith availeth;
Pray, brethren, pray,
On God your spirit stay.

Chorus:

A few more days of sorrow
And the Lord will call us home,
To walk the golden streets
Of the new Jerusalem.

Sing, brethren, sing,
Rejoice with joy and gladness;
Sing, brethren, sing,
Your songs of triumph bring.

(Chorus)

Rise, brethren, rise,
Your souls by faith ascending;
Rise, brethren, rise,
Your home is in the skies.

(Chorus)
