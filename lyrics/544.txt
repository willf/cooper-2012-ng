Title: My Eternal Home
Alternative Title: Home
Page: 544
Tune Composer: Montague, W. L.; Hughes, H. D. (arranger)
Tune Composition Date: 1866; 1927 (arrangement)
Lyrics: Christian Harmony
Lyrics Date: 1866
Meter: Short Meter (6,6,8,6)

________________________________________________________________________________

My Father’s house on high,
Is my eternal home,
O God forbid that I should fail,
While trav’ling here below.

Chorus:

Then join the heav’nly throng,
To sing redeeming love,
While endless ages roll along,
We’ll praise our God above.

My Father and my God,
Oh lead me safely on,
Till in that heav’nly world above,
I feel my work is done.

(Chorus)
