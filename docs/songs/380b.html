Lyrics

Oh Lord, look down from Thy throne,
Have mercy on us here.
Teach us, Lord, to look to Thee,
And Thy name to revere.
Keep us, Lord, in all Thy ways,
Lead us in Thy right paths.
Call us, Lord to live with Thee,
And in Thy smiles to bask.
