Song · Page 537

Praise the Lord

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

I am walking with my Savior ev’ry day,
Praise the Lord, O praise the Lord;
And He’s promised to go with me all the way,
Praise the Lord, O praise the Lord.

Chorus:

Praise the Lord on high, O praise the Lord on high,
Hallelujah! praise ye the Lord on high.

When I’m weary then He gives me blessed rest,
Praise the Lord, O praise the Lord;
And in tender love He holds me to His breast,
Praise the Lord, O praise the Lord.

(Chorus)

Soon He’ll lead me home to glory ne’er to roam,
Praise the Lord, O praise the Lord;
To that place of blessing on the happy shore,
Praise the Lord, O praise the Lord.

(Chorus)
