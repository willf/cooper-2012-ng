Song · Page 562

On To Calvary

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

I love to think of my dear Lord,
For He has done so much for me,
He’s shown me to the narrow road,
That leads me on to Calvary.

Now, in a song of grateful praise,
To my dear Lord, my voice I’ll raise;
With all His saints, I’ll join to tell,
That Jesus hath done all things well.
