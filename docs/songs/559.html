Song · Page 559

Will You Meet Me?

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

I am going home today
In the narrow gospel way;
Soon I’ll be at rest above,
There to sing redeeming love.

Chorus:

With the angels I shall stand,
In that holy happy land,
Brother, will you meet me there?
Heaven’s joys with me to share.

Jesus will me pilot be,
O’er the dark uncertain sea;
With all pain and sorrow past,
I will anchor safe at last.

(Chorus)

In my blessed Savior’s arms,
I’ll be safe from all alarms;
From all suff’ring I’ll be free,
Brother will you be with me?

(Chorus)
