Gone to Heaven

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

Oh, where is my father?
He is gone to his home,
He’s where we all may meet in heaven,
Where the wicked cease from troubling,
And the weary are at rest.

Oh, where is my mother?
She is gone to her home,
She’s where we all may meet in heaven,
Where the wicked cease from troubling,
And the weary are at rest.

Oh, where is my Savior?
He is gone to His home,
He’s where we all may meet in heaven,
Where the wicked cease from troubling,
And the weary are at rest.
