I Love to Sing of Jesus

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

I love to sing of Jesus,
And of the wondrous plan,
He has laid for our salvation,
Redeeming sinful man.

Chorus:

He is Alpha, and Omega,
Let the people praise the Lord from shore to shore.
Hallelujah, hallelujah,
Praise the Lord, O praise the Lord for evermore.

I love to read of beauties
In Revelation’s Book,
When the doors of heaven opened,
And John did take a look.

(Chorus)
