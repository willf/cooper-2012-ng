Guiding Spirit

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

Our life is ever on the wing,
And death is ever nigh,
The moment when our life begins
We all begin to die.

Chorus:

Then the eternal spirit guides,
Our souls by day and night,
And when we reach the heav’nly land
Our souls shall be at rest.
