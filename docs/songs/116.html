Song · Page 116

I Will Shout and Sing in Glory

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

O who will come and go with me?
I am bound for the promised land,
I’m bound fair Canaan’s land to see,
I am bound for the promised land.

Chorus:

I will shout His praise in glory,
So will I, so will I,
And we’ll all sing hallelujah by and by.

My heav’nly home is bright and fair,
I am bound for the promised land,
No pain or death can enter there,
I am bound for the promised land.

(Chorus)

Its glitt’ring towers the sun outshine,
I am bound for the promised land,
That heav’nly mansion shall be mine,
I am bound for the promised land.

(Chorus)

My Father’s house is built on high,
I am bound for the promised land,
Far, far above the starry sky,
I am bound for the promised land.

(Chorus)
