Fallen by the Way

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

I feel I’ve fallen by the way,
I feel I’m all undone;
My way gets shorter ev’ry day
To my eternal home.

Chorus:

Jesus, blessed Savior,
Hear my fainting cry;
O Jesus, be thou nearer
When I am called to die.
