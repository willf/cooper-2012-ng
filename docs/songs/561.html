Calling Me

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

Yes, I think I hear my Saviour calling me,
And I think I see Him hanging on a tree;
O the Lamb of Calvary, and His blood He shed for me,
And I know my Savior’s calling now.

Chorus:

Calling now, calling now,
Yes I love to think of my Jesus as my Friend,
Calling now, calling now,
He has promised to be with us to the end.
