Canaan’s Peaceful Shore

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

I’m on my journey home,
To Canaan’s peaceful shore,
Where troubles never come
And partings are no more.

Chorus:

O Canaan, sweet Canaan,
I’m bound for Canaan’s land to dwell.

When earthly cares are o’er,
On angel’s wings I’ll fly,
To Canaan’s happy shore,
A brighter world on high.

(Chorus)
