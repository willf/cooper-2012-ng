Fleeting Days

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

Time! what an empty vapor ’tis!
Our days, how swift they are,’
Swift as an Indian arrow flies,
Or like a shooting star.

Our life is ever on the wing,
And death is ever nigh;
The moment when our lives begin,
We all begin to die.
