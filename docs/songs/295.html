The Sinner Must Be Born Again

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

Awaked by Sinai’s awful sound,
My soul in guilt and thrall I found,
And knew not where to go;
O’erwhelmed with sin, with anguish slain,
The sinner must be born again
Or sink in endless woe.
