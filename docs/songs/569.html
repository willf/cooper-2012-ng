He Leads Me All the Way

LyricsSong details

Lyrics

My Savior leads me ev’ry day,
Through life’s long weary road;
He guides my feet along the way,
And to the throne of grace.
My Savior leads me ev’ry day,
Thro’ ev’ry trial and fear;
Is ever near, my heart to cheer,
He leads me all the way.
