Lyrics

Awake and sing the song,
Of Moses and the Lamb,
Tune ev’ry heart and ev’ry tongue,
To praise the Savior’s name.

Soon shall we hear Him say,
‘Ye blessed children, come!’
Soon will He call us hence away
To our eternal home.
