Am I a soldier of the cross,
A foll’wer of the Lamb?
And shall I fear to own His cause,
Or blush to speak His name?

Chorus:

O the Lamb, the living Lamb,
The Lamb on Calvary,
The Lamb that was slain, but lives again,
To intercede for me.
