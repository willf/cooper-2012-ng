Come On, My Friends

Lyrics

I long to reach the shore of bliss,
And see the New Jerusalem,

Chorus:

Come on, come on, my friends, come on,
The gospel sounds the jubilee.

Where my beloved Jesus is,
And spend eternity with Him.

(Chorus)
