Religion Is Sweet

Lyrics

’Tis religion that can give
Sweetest pleasures while we live,
’Tis religion must supply
Solid comforts when we die.

After death, its joys will be
Lasting as eternity;
Be the living God my friend,
Then my bliss shall never end.
